Mounting & Framing Options

Every piece can be customized with our workshop framing solutions to seamlessly integrate into your home or heritage property.

Solid Scottish Oak Surround

Sustainably harvested Scottish oak with hand-rubbed beeswax finish. Includes countersunk keyhole brass wall mounts.

  • Deep shadow-line profile
  • Natural, dark fumed, or blackened oak
  • Interior hanging or window sill placement

Patinated Brass Perimeter

Solid extruded brass channel came chemically oxidized to an antique bronze patina for an understated museum-grade display.

  • Ultra-slim 10mm sightline
  • Corrosion-resistant antique finish
  • Integrated top suspension loops

Suspended Steel Cable System

Minimalist floating suspension for window apertures, utilizing marine-grade stainless steel cables and micro-tensioners.

  • Virtually invisible ceiling fixings
  • Rated to 45kg load capacity
  • Allows light to pass unobstructed
