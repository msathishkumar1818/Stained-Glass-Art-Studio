The Creation Journey

The 5-Stage Commission Journey

Every bespoke stained glass commission follows our time-honoured atelier methodology, ensuring precision engineering and archival permanence.

01

Site & Light Survey

We analyze aperture dimensions, compass orientation, seasonal daylight intensity, and surrounding architectural mouldings.

Week 1 • Consultation
02

Full-Scale Cartoons

Our artists hand-paint 1:1 scale watercolor cartoons with exact cutlines and glass color swatches for client approval.

Weeks 2–3 • Design Phase
03

Hand Glass Scoring

Mouthblown antique glass sheets are individually scored and grozed to match pattern templates with sub-millimeter tolerances.

Weeks 4–5 • Atelier Craft
04

Leading & Cementation

Pieces are knitted into heavy lead came, joints soldered, and pressurized with black glazing cement for weatherproof rigidity.

Week 6 • Glazing Bench
05

Master Installation

Our team fits the panel on-site with bespoke brass or bronze glazing saddles, optional secondary protective glazing, and heritage warranty.

Week 7 • Handover
