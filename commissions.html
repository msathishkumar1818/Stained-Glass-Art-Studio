The Creation Journey

The 5-Stage Commission Journey

Our 5-stage atelier process ensures precision craftsmanship and lasting permanence.

01

Site & Light Survey

We assess dimensions, compass orientation, seasonal daylight, and architectural framing.

Week 1 • Consultation
02

Full-Scale Cartoons

Hand-painted 1:1 scale watercolor cartoons with cutlines and glass color swatches.

Weeks 2–3 • Design Phase
03

Hand Glass Scoring

Mouthblown antique glass sheets scored and grozed to match templates precisely.

Weeks 4–5 • Atelier Craft
04

Leading & Cementation

Fitted into lead came, hand-soldered at joints, and cemented for weatherproof rigidity.

Week 6 • Glazing Bench
05

Master Installation

On-site precision installation with custom saddles, protective glazing, and warranty.

Week 7 • Handover
